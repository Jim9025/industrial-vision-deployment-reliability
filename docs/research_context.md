# Research context and evidence boundary

The related master's work concerns a frozen industrial anomaly detector after
image acquisition conditions change. A predicted PASS may still be a False OK.
It studies model evaluation, bounded human review, and what can be inferred from
limited evidence. The sequence PASS yield → capacity → ranking → finite-evidence
identifiability is a **post-seal conceptual synthesis**, not a pre-specified
four-stage experimental design.

The sealed Fabric and Rice evaluations primarily exhibited capacity-limited
regimes. One sealed Real-IAD cross-view evaluation exposed a zero-PASS
operating-point boundary. The theoretical statement is conditional on its
assumptions. These observations do not establish universal ranking failure,
general external validation of the whole framework, or safe factory deployment.

The full thesis and internal audits are excluded pending advisor assessment.
